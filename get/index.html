Jisho

An offline Japanese dictionary with kanji stroke order, handwriting search and flashcards.

Download for Android (v1.0.2)

iPhone, iPad or computer? Open Jisho in the browser and add it to your home screen.

Install

  1. Tap the button above in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. When asked, allow Chrome to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Jisho — it downloads the dictionary once (~40 MB) and then works offline.
New versions arrive inside the app automatically.

Decks

  • JLPT N5 starter 60 cards
    60 everyday words to begin with. Readings and meanings are filled in from the dictionary when left empty.
Add them in the app under Flashcards.
