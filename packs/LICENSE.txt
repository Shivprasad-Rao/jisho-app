Jisho dictionary data: sources and licenses
===========================================

Jisho's dictionary packs (core-en, lang-*, ja-explain) are adapted from the
works below. We changed them: we converted them to SQLite, merged, trimmed and
re-indexed them for offline search, and added frequency/priority rankings.
The packs are shared under the same licenses as their sources (ShareAlike):
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), except for the parts
listed below under other licenses. The data comes WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY.

- JMdict (Japanese-multilingual dictionary), KANJIDIC2, RADKFILE and KRADFILE
  Copyright (c) James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and
  Development Group (EDRDG). Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
  https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
  Obtained in JSON form from jmdict-simplified by Mikhail Scheglov (scriptin)
  https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified

- Example sentences: Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), by the Tatoeba
  contributors. Licensed CC BY 2.0 FR
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). They come through JMdict
  and jmdict-simplified.

- Kanji stroke order: KanjiVG, Copyright (c) 2009-2024 Ulrich Apel.
  Licensed CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
  https://kanjivg.tagaini.net

- Japanese explanations (ja-explain): Japanese Wiktionary
  (https://ja.wiktionary.org), by the Wiktionary contributors. Licensed
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (text also available under the GNU FDL). Extracted by
  Wiktextract / kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org), by Tatu Ylonen.
  Each entry's history and authors are available on its Wiktionary page.

- Handwriting recognition patterns: KanjiCanvas, Copyright (c) 2019 Dominik
  Klein, MIT license (see the software notices).
  http://github.com/asdfjkl/kanjicanvas
